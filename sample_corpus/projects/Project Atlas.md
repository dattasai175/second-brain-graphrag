# Project Atlas

Project Atlas is the event streaming platform at [[Northwind Labs]]. It is owned by [[Priya Raman]] and its budget was approved by [[Marcus Lee]].

## Architecture

Atlas is built on Apache Kafka, chosen in [[RFC-12 Event Streaming]]. Producers publish to regional clusters and consumers replay topics from any offset.

## Timeline

The pilot launched in the third quarter of 2025. General availability is planned for the second quarter of 2026.
